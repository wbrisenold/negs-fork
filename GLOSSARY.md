# Negs Image Rendering

Terms for discussing camera rendering looks and the color transformations applied to scanned photographs.

## Color rendering

**HNCS**:
Hasselblad Natural Colour Solution, Hasselblad's proprietary colour-management and rendering system for its camera pipeline. The public description names a Hasselblad LUT, film curve, and additional colour processing, but does not publish the complete algorithm.
_Avoid_: treating any single post-render filter as the complete HNCS system.

**HNCS look**:
A software rendering intended to evoke the appearance associated with HNCS. It is an approximation of a visual style and does not mean the image was rendered through Hasselblad's camera-specific colour pipeline.
_Avoid_: HNCS implementation, when referring to an approximation.
